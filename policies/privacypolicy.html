Privacy Policy

How MakeThisQuick collects, uses and protects information — on this site, in client work, and in the tools we connect to your accounts.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Who we are

MakeThisQuick LLC (“MakeThisQuick”, “we”, “us”) is a digital transformation studio. We provide strategy and consulting, custom software and automation, brand and website design, business-formation help, and ongoing management of websites and advertising accounts for small businesses. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers makethisquick.com (the “Site”), the services we deliver to clients, and the internal tools we use to deliver them.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you contact us, book a consultation, subscribe to our newsletter, or become a client, we collect what you provide: your name, email address, phone number, business name, and the details of your business and goals that you share with us. Clients also provide the content, images, logins and account access needed to deliver the work.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the Site we collect standard device and usage information — IP address, browser type, time zone, pages viewed, the site or search that referred you, and how you interact with the Site — using cookies, log files and analytics tags. We use Google Analytics for this; you can read Google’s privacy policy at google.com/policies/privacy and opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Payment information. Payments are processed by third-party payment processors (such as Stripe). We receive confirmation of payment and the last four digits of a card; we do not store full card numbers. Where a client pays a platform directly (for example, a Google Ads account billed to the client’s own card), we never hold that payment method.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and provide the services you engage us for.
  • To set up, build, host, manage and report on client websites, software and advertising accounts.
  • To invoice, process payments and keep the records the law requires.
  • To send service messages about your engagement, and — only if you subscribed — occasional emails about how small businesses are using AI. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To understand how the Site is used and improve it.
  • To protect against fraud and abuse and to comply with legal obligations.

Client data and client customers

When we build or manage a website, form, advertising account or analytics property for a client, we act as a service provider on that client’s behalf. Information submitted by the client’s customers (for example, a contact-form enquiry on a site we built) belongs to the client and is governed by the client’s own privacy policy. We access it only to deliver and support the service, and we never use one client’s data for another client, for our own marketing, or to train artificial-intelligence models.

Google account data and connected services

MakeThisQuick operates internal tools (including the application registered with Google as “MakeThisQuick-AdsAutomation”) that connect to Google services on behalf of our clients. These tools are used only by MakeThisQuick staff to deliver the marketing and web services a client has engaged us for. They are not offered to the public and no member of the public is asked to sign in to them.

When a client, or a member of our team acting for a client, authorises one of these tools with a Google account, the tool may access the following through Google’s APIs: Google Ads account data (campaigns, keywords, ads, budgets, performance metrics and conversion data); Google Analytics property data and configuration; Google Search Console data and site verification; and, where a client has asked us to manage it, Google Business Profile information. We use this data solely to set up, manage, measure and report on that client’s own advertising and website performance, and to produce the reports we deliver to that client.

We do not sell Google user data, do not use it for advertising purposes other than the client’s own campaigns, do not use it to build or train generalised machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models, and do not share it with any third party except the service providers that host our tooling and the client whose data it is. One client’s data is never combined with, or made visible to, another client. Access tokens are stored on MakeThisQuick-controlled devices and are revoked when an engagement ends; report data is retained for the length of the engagement and for up to twelve months afterwards for record-keeping unless the client asks us to delete it sooner.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke our access at any time from your Google Account’s third-party access settings or by emailing us at the address below.

AI tools

We use AI assistants and automation in our own work — drafting, research, building and reporting. Client information is shared with these tools only as needed to do the work, under provider terms that do not permit the provider to train on it. We do not upload client customer lists, payment details or health information to AI tools.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with the service providers we need to run the business and deliver the work: website hosting and domain registrars, payment processors, email and document tools, analytics and advertising platforms (Google), and AI and development tooling. Each acts on our instructions. We also disclose information when the law requires it — to comply with a subpoena or other lawful request — or to protect our rights. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Cookies and advertising

The Site uses cookies for analytics. We may run our own advertising on Google and social platforms and use their conversion measurement; you can opt out of personalised advertising at google.com/settings/ads, Facebook ad settings and optout.aboutads.info. We do not currently respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept for as long as we are in conversation and up to two years after. Client records, invoices and work product are kept for the length of the engagement and for as long as tax and accounting rules require afterwards (generally seven years for financial records). Analytics data follows the retention setting of the platform, and newsletter details until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to a particular use. Residents of California, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other places with privacy laws have specific rights under those laws, and we honour them regardless of where you live. To exercise any of these, email us at the address below; we will respond within 30 days. If you are outside the United States, be aware that your information is processed in the United States.

Children

Our services are for businesses and their owners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services or the law change. The date at the top tells you when it was last revised; material changes will be announced on the Site.
