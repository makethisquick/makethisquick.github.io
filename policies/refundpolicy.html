Refund Policy

Short version: custom work is paid for as it is done; subscriptions cancel any time and stop at the end of the period.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Project work

Our work is custom and labour-based. Payments for work performed are not refundable, and deposits are non-refundable once work has started. If you cancel a project before it is finished, you pay for the work completed to that point at the agreed rate and we hand over the deliverables you own under our Terms of Service (section 7).

Subscriptions and hosted plans

Any minimum term stated at checkout is charged up front and is non-refundable. After that, plans renew automatically until you cancel. Cancel any time from your account portal or by email; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, nothing is charged after that, and partial periods are not refunded.

Advertising spend

Media spend is billed by the advertising platform to your own payment method and is governed by that platform’s terms. Our management fee is not refundable for periods already delivered.

If we got it wrong

If we failed to deliver something we committed to in writing, tell us within 14 days. We will fix it or refund the part of the fee that relates to it.
